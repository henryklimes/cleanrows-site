Find company emails and phone numbers from a website

How to collect role addresses (info@, sales@) and phone numbers from a company's own website, what not to scrape, and a hosted Actor that reads the site instead of a third-party database.

If you need a way to contact a company, the company's own website is the most honest source. Role inboxes such as info@, sales@, press@ and support@, plus a click-to-call number in the footer or on the contact page, are meant to be found. A third-party enrichment database is a snapshot that may be months old and may include personal mailboxes the company never published.

What to try for free

For a short list of domains you can:

  1. Fetch the homepage, then follow obvious Contact, Imprint / Legal and About links (at most a handful of pages).
  2. Collect mailto: and tel: links, schema.org Organization emails and telephones, and addresses that appear in footer or contact copy.
  3. Keep only role addresses on the company's own domain by default. Named personal mailboxes need a lawful basis to process; "it was on the website" is not automatically enough.
  4. Ignore emails on other domains (a SaaS vendor, a newsletter host) and ignore phone numbers that belong to a shared call-tracking pool if you cannot tell them apart.

This falls down when the company hides the address behind a form, when the contact page is rendered only in JavaScript, or when you have hundreds of domains to do on a schedule.

A hosted option

Company Website Enricher takes a list of domains and reads the company's own site (homepage, contact/imprint and about pages, respecting robots.txt). It returns business emails, phone numbers, a postal address when one is marked up, social profiles, a logo and a rough tech stack. By default only role addresses are kept (onlyGenericEmails: true). Price: $4 per 1,000 companies.

import os
from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient(os.environ["APIFY_TOKEN"])
run = client.actor("henryklimes/company-enricher").call(run_input={
    "domains": ["stripe.com", "notion.so"],
    "onlyGenericEmails": True,
    "detectAts": False,
})
for row in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    print(row["domain"], row.get("emails"), row.get("phones"))

It does not invent a number or inbox the company never published. Empty fields usually mean the site uses a form instead of a public address. For logos and social profiles see Get a company logo and social links from a domain. The broader enrichment guide is Company website enrichment.

FAQ

Is this the same as buying a contact database?

No. The Actor (and the DIY steps above) only read what the company currently publishes on its own website. They will not estimate headcount, revenue or a mobile number that is not on the site.

Should I collect personal staff emails?

Only if you have a lawful basis to process them. The default is to keep role inboxes such as info@ and sales@ and to drop named personal addresses.

Why is the email field empty?

Many companies no longer print an address and send you to a form, Intercom, or a "contact us" tool. If it is not on the pages that were fetched, the field stays empty.
