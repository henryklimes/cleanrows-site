Check if a company runs Google Ads, and export competitor ads

How to see whether a company is running Google Ads and export those ads — format, run dates and images — from the Google Ads Transparency Center, in bulk.

Google publishes every advertiser's Search, YouTube and Display ads in the Ads Transparency Center. That website is fine for checking one brand by hand. It is a poor place to score a list of leads, or to export a competitor's ads with dates and creatives into a spreadsheet. Google's official Transparency Center API only covers ads served in the EU/EEA, and only for approved callers.

What you can get

From a domain, a brand name or a Google advertiser ID:

  • Advertiser check — whether the company is advertising, Google's total ad-count range, the advertiser accounts behind the ads, the latest ad date, and a mix of formats (text, image, video).
  • Ads — one row per ad: format, first and last shown dates, days running, a creative image or preview, and a link back to the Transparency Center.

You can filter by country and by ads shown in the last N days. Setting ads per query to 0 returns only the check, which is the cheap way to score a lead list.

Limits worth knowing

  • Ad copy text is not extracted. Image ads and many text ads include an image URL; some responsive and video ads only have Google's preview page.
  • Domain searches return every advertiser whose ads point at that domain, which can include resellers and partners. Use an advertiser ID when you want one account.
  • Google publishes ad counts as ranges (for example 1000-2000). Small counts are exact.
  • Brand names are matched as whole words and, if needed, resolved to the brand's website. A name that matches nothing comes back as not_found and is free.

The Actor

Google Ads Library Scraper (Ads Transparency Center) does the brand resolution, paging and country filters. It costs $0.50 per 1,000 ads and $2 per 1,000 company checks, with no start fee. Not-found and failed lookups are free.

Example tasks:

  • Who runs Google Ads now (lead list check)
  • Competitor video ads in Germany
  • All ads of an advertiser, last 90 days
