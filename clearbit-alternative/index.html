Clearbit alternative: enrich companies from their website

A Clearbit alternative that reads emails, phones, socials, logo and tech stack from the company's own website, not a third-party database.

Clearbit popularised "give me a domain, get a company profile." Since then the product has been sold and repriced, and people now search for a Clearbit alternative that still starts from a website. There are two different jobs in that sentence, and it helps to split them.

A database enrichment (Clearbit, Apollo, many data brokers) matches the domain to a record they already store: headcount, funding, a named contact. That is convenient, and it is also stale the moment the company changes its site. A website enrichment fetches the company's own pages and reads what they currently publish: a role inbox, a phone number, a LinkedIn URL, a logo, a CMS or analytics fingerprint.

If you need "who is the VP of Sales," a people database is the category. If you need "what this company says about itself today, plus a way to email the company rather than a person," reading the site is the more honest source.

Doing it yourself

Fetch the homepage, follow Contact / About / Imprint, parse mailto:, tel links and JSON-LD, and keep only role addresses (info@, hello@, press@) if you do not want personal inboxes. Honour robots.txt. This is fine for a short list and tedious, fragile work at a few hundred domains.

FAQ

Is this a drop-in Clearbit API replacement?

No. It does not return funded headcount, org charts or named employees from a commercial database. It returns what the company's own website publishes. If you need people data, you still need a people product.

Does it return personal email addresses?

Not by default. onlyGenericEmails keeps role inboxes. Turn that off only if you have a lawful reason to collect personal addresses.

Can I enrich a Google Maps or local-business list?

Yes. The Enrich local business websites from a Google Maps list task is built for that input.
