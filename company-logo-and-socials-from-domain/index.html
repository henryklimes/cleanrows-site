Get a company logo and social links from a domain

How to find a company's logo, LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, GitHub and TikTok URLs from its own website, and a hosted Actor that returns them as one profile per domain.

A company domain is usually enough to recover the logo and the official social profiles. The homepage, the footer and a few meta tags are the right place to look. Directory sites and enrichment databases often lag behind a rebrand, or they mix in share-button links that are not the company's own account.

What to try for free

For a short list of domains:

  1. Fetch the homepage (and, if needed, the About page).
  2. Prefer a logo from schema.org Organization.logo, apple-touch-icon, or og:image only when it is clearly the mark rather than a generic social card.
  3. Collect official profile URLs from footer and header links: LinkedIn /company/…, X, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, GitHub and TikTok.
  4. Drop share buttons (?share=, "Tweet this", "Share on LinkedIn") and links that point at an employee or a random tag, not at the company.

A dozen domains are a quiet afternoon. A few hundred, with consistent field names and fewer false share-button links, is a small pipeline.

A hosted option

Company Website Enricher returns logo_url and a socials object (linkedin, x, facebook, instagram, youtube, github, tiktok) for each domain, read from the company's own site. Share buttons are ignored. You also get name, description, emails and phones when they are published. Price: $4 per 1,000 companies.

import os
from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient(os.environ["APIFY_TOKEN"])
run = client.actor("henryklimes/company-enricher").call(run_input={
    "domains": ["bunq.com", "notion.so"],
    "onlyGenericEmails": True,
})
for row in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    socials = row.get("socials") or {}
    print(row["domain"], row.get("logo_url"), socials.get("linkedin"))

It will not invent a logo or a profile the company does not link. For emails and phone numbers see Find company emails and phone numbers from a website. The broader guide is Company website enrichment.

FAQ

Where does the logo URL come from?

From the company's own pages, usually schema.org Organization.logo or a site icon. It is a URL, not a downloaded file. If the company only uses an image inside a scripted header, the field may be empty.

How do you avoid share-button links?

Links that are clearly "share this page" actions are ignored. Only profiles that look like the company's own account are kept. If a site only has share buttons, the social field stays empty.

Can I get a Facebook or LinkedIn profile that is not linked from the website?

Not with this method. If the company does not publish the profile on its own site, you will not get it here. Guessing a LinkedIn slug from the brand name is unreliable.
