AI tools search interest in the US, week to 23 November 2026

Snapshot taken on 23 November 2026 at 09:35 CET, Europe/Amsterdam. Written by Cleanrows.

Snapshot of Google Trends taken on 23 November 2026 at 09:35 CET. Data collected with Google Trends API & Scraper – Bulk Keywords, Compare, Trending by Cleanrows, in one compare run (8 keyword-rows, $0.002 in Actor events at the public price plus under $0.001 of platform usage, about 0.05 CZK). These are relative search-interest figures, not product usage, installs or revenue.

What we measured

The same eight AI-tool search terms as in the 21 November snapshot, web search, United States, time range today 1-m (23 October to 23 November 2026), all on one shared 0–100 scale:

ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude AI, Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity AI, Grok, Midjourney, Cursor AI.

Related queries were switched off this time; this is a short weekly check of the ranking. 23 November is marked partial by Google and is left out of the day-level numbers.

Key numbers

  • ChatGPT still dominates. Average interest 78.26 on the shared scale; its peak of 100 is now 10 November. Google Gemini, in second place, averages 7.19.
  • Grok moved ahead of Claude AI. Two days ago the two were tied at 5.03. Today Grok averages 4.74 and Claude AI 4.64, so Grok ranks third and Claude AI fourth. The gap is small (0.1 points) and both are within normal week-to-week noise.
  • Week over week, on the same scale: comparing the last 7 complete days (16–22 November) with the 7 days before (9–15 November), Google Gemini rose 3.7% (7.71 → 8.00) and Claude AI 3.0% (4.85 → 4.99). ChatGPT fell 1.8% (81.29 → 79.86) and Grok 3.0% (4.71 → 4.57).
  • Microsoft Copilot fell back after its 12 November spike. Its 7-day average dropped from 1.00 to 0.71 (−28.6%). The 12 November peak (2 on the shared scale) was tied to outage searches in the last report; it is still the term's highest day in the window.
  • Weekends stay quiet. ChatGPT was 56 on both Saturday 21 and Sunday 22 November, versus 81–92 on weekdays the week before. The same weekday/weekend pattern shows for every term.
  • Direction labels barely changed. Gemini is still the only rising term (+14.3%). Grok, Perplexity AI, Midjourney and Cursor AI are falling; ChatGPT, Claude AI and Copilot are stable. Copilot and Cursor AI remain precision: low next to a term that peaks at 100.

change_percent compares the average of the last quarter of the timeline with the first quarter. trend_direction is the Actor's label from that figure.

How to reproduce it

import os
from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient(os.environ["APIFY_TOKEN"])

run = client.actor("henryklimes/google-trends").call(run_input={
    "mode": "compare",
    "keywords": [
        "ChatGPT", "Google Gemini", "Claude AI", "Microsoft Copilot",
        "Perplexity AI", "Grok", "Midjourney", "Cursor AI",
    ],
    "geos": ["US"],
    "timeRange": "today 1-m",
    "includeRegions": False,
    "includeRelatedQueries": False,
})
for row in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    print(row["rank"], row["keyword"], row["average_interest"], row["trend_direction"])

The ready-made task Compare more than 5 keywords in Google Trends runs the same mode with your own keywords. The Actor charges $0.25 per 1,000 keyword-rows that return data (no_data and failed rows are free); this run returned 8 rows.

Limits of this snapshot

  • Google Trends is relative interest, not search volume.
  • One country (US), one month, eight named queries. It is not "the AI tools market".
  • Day-level values for small terms are rounded by Google, so changes of a few hundredths are noise.
  • I built the Actor this report uses. The numbers are still just what the run returned.
