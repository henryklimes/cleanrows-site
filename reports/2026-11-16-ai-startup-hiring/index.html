AI startup hiring report — 2026-11-16

Snapshot taken on 16 November 2026 at 09:37 CET, Europe/Amsterdam. Written by Cleanrows.

Snapshot of public job boards taken on 16 November 2026 at 09:37 CET. Data collected with ATS Jobs Feed by Cleanrows. Every number below comes from that single run (the change section also uses the previous snapshot); the raw dataset is attached as CSV.

Key numbers

  • 6,481 open job postings across 39 companies: 3,157 at 17 AI companies and 3,324 at 22 other well-known, mostly YC-backed startups (Scale AI is on both preset lists and is counted once, in the AI group).
  • Four companies hold 49% of all postings: Databricks (895), OpenAI (838), Stripe (785), Anthropic (661).
  • Engineering is the largest function with 2,360 postings (36%). At the AI companies, engineering plus research and ML make up 58% of postings; at the startup group it is 35%.
  • Sales and go-to-market is second with 1,276 postings (20%).
  • 184 "forward deployed" roles are open, led by Databricks (83), OpenAI (36), Scale AI (12), Cohere (10).
  • 108 job titles mention "agent" (for example AI agents or agent platforms).
  • 35% of postings (2,288) list remote work as an option, 31% at the AI companies and 40% in the startup group.
  • San Francisco is listed 2,247 times in posting locations, New York 1,238 times; London (294) is the top city outside the US.

What changed since the last report (15 November 2026 at 13:11 CET)

The previous snapshot is less than a day old, so this is a day-over-day view, not a weekly trend. Postings are matched by ATS and job ID.

  • Total open postings: 6,493 → 6,481 (-12).
  • 10 postings were added and 22 were taken down.
  • Added: GitLab (4), Flexport (2), OpenAI (1), Databricks (1), Airbnb (1), Benchling (1).
  • Taken down: Anthropic (7), Airbnb (5), OpenAI (3), Glean (2), Databricks (1), Ramp (1).
  • The ranking of the top four companies, the function mix and the remote share did not change in any meaningful way.

New in the last 7 days (Ashby boards only)

Ashby reports a publish date for every posting, so recency can be measured for the 14 companies on Ashby (Anyscale, Benchling, Character.AI, Cohere, Midjourney, Modal, Mux, Notion, OpenAI, Perplexity, Plaid, Ramp, Runway (board now shows cfo.ai), Snyk). Greenhouse's public board API only reports a last-updated date, so Greenhouse companies are left out of this section.

  • 179 of 1,777 Ashby postings (10%) were published in the last 7 days, and 562 (32%) in the last 30 days.
  • Most new postings in the last 7 days: OpenAI (94), Ramp (24), Notion (19), Perplexity (15), Cohere (10), Benchling (9).

Method

  • Source: each company's public job board API on Greenhouse (4,704 postings) or Ashby (1,777). No login, no scraping of personal data.
  • Companies: the ai-companies preset list plus the startup list in marketing/scripts/report_input.json. These are curated lists, not a full market sample, so the numbers describe these companies only. The Ashby board Runway now shows the company name cfo.ai; it stays in the AI group as listed in the preset.
  • Counting: one row per posting as listed by the company. Some companies post the same role once per location, which raises their count.
  • Run: one ATS Jobs Feed run on 2026-11-16, about 4 minutes, $0.040 of Apify platform usage (compute plus dataset writes). Re-run it yourself with the AI startup jobs task.
  • Dataset: reports/data/ai-startup-hiring-2026-11-16.csv with company, group, ATS, job ID, title, function, department, location, remote flag, employment type, dates and apply URL.
