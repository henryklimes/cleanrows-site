Company website enrichment from the company's own site

Enrich a company domain with name, role emails, phones, social profiles, tech stack, careers page and ATS — read from the website itself, not a third-party database.

Most "company enrichment" products sell a record from a third-party database: a snapshot of emails, headcount and socials that may be months out of date, and that the company itself did not publish in that shape. If what you need is what the company currently prints on its own website, you can read the site instead.

What a website actually gives you

From the homepage, the contact page, the footer and a few well-known paths you can usually extract:

  • company name, a short description, a logo;
  • business emails and phone numbers (role inboxes such as sales@, not a scraped personal mailbox);
  • a postal address when it is published;
  • LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and GitHub links;
  • a rough tech stack from public script origins and meta tags;
  • the contact page, the careers page, the ATS if one is linked, and a count of open jobs.

This will not replace a credit-style data vendor. It will not invent a mobile number the company never published. It will tell you what is true on the site today.
