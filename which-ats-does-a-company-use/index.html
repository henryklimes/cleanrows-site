Which ATS does a company use? Bulk lookup from a domain

Find a company's careers page, the applicant tracking system behind it, the job board ID and how many jobs are open, from a list of domains.

If you are building a hiring-signal tool, a job board, or an outreach list, the first question is often not "what jobs are open" but "where does this company post them?" The answer is an applicant tracking system (ATS) and a board ID. Guessing from the company name is unreliable: the Greenhouse token is not always the brand, and many companies hide the careers page a click or two off the homepage.

A reasonable DIY approach

For a short list you can:

  1. fetch the company homepage and follow obvious "Careers" / "Jobs" links;
  2. look at the destination host (boards.greenhouse.io, jobs.lever.co, jobs.ashbyhq.com, *.myworkdayjobs.com, and so on);
  3. parse the board ID out of the path;
  4. optionally call that ATS's public board API to count openings.

This falls down on JavaScript-rendered career pages, unusual ATS products, and anything at a few hundred domains. It also does not tell you about 20 of the less common systems.

The Actor

Careers Page & ATS Detector takes a list of company domains and returns, per domain, the careers page URL, the ATS (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee, BambooHR, Personio, Teamtailor and 15 more), the job-board ID, and the number of open jobs. $3 per 1,000 domains.

It detects the ATS and counts jobs. It does not download every posting. For the postings themselves, use ATS Jobs Feed or Workday Jobs Feed with the board ID this Actor found.

Example tasks:

  • Find which ATS a company uses
  • Find careers pages for a list of company domains
  • Count open jobs per company for sales prospecting
