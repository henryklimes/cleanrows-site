Export Google Trends related queries and rising search terms

How to pull top and rising related queries (including Breakout terms) from Google Trends, why unofficial clients often get a 429, and a hosted option that returns them as rows.

Related queries are the most useful part of Google Trends for keyword research. For a seed term you get two lists: top (0–100, relative to the most common related search) and rising (percent change versus the previous period, or the label "Breakout" when the term is new or the increase is huge). Related topics exist in the Trends interface too, but automated clients often get an empty list.

What to try for free

  1. Use the Trends website. Open trends.google.com, enter one keyword, pick a geography and a time range, and scroll to Related queries. You can download a CSV for that one view.
  2. Call the unofficial explore widgets, which is what libraries such as pytrends wrap (related_queries()). Batch at most five keywords, send a normal browser cookie, and back off on HTTP 429. The pytrends repository is archived, so you will own any breakage.
  3. Do not depend on related topics. Google frequently returns that widget empty for scripted requests. Treat related_topics as best-effort.
  4. Cache the lists. Rising queries do not need a refresh every hour. A daily or weekly job is enough for most content calendars.

A short list is fine by hand. The work starts when you want related queries for hundreds of keywords, several countries, or a scheduled refresh from a shared cloud IP that Google already rate-limits.

A hosted option

Google Trends API & Scraper – Bulk Keywords, Compare, Trending returns related_queries_top and related_queries_rising on each keyword row, with an is_breakout flag. Related topics are off by default; you can turn them on, and related_topics_available tells you whether Google actually sent any. Price: $0.25 per 1,000 keyword-rows with data. Failed and no_data rows are not charged.

import os
from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient(os.environ["APIFY_TOKEN"])
run = client.actor("henryklimes/google-trends").call(run_input={
    "mode": "keyword",
    "keywords": ["heat pump", "induction hob"],
    "geos": ["US"],
    "timeRange": "today 3-m",
    "includeRelatedQueries": True,
    "includeRelatedTopics": False,
    "includeRegions": False,
})
for row in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    rising = row.get("related_queries_rising") or []
    print(row["keyword"], [q["query"] for q in rising[:5]])

FAQ

Are related-query values search volumes?

No. Top related queries are a 0–100 index within that list. Rising values are a percent change, or "Breakout" when Google does not give a percentage.

Why is the related-topics list empty?

The topics widget often comes back empty for automated clients, even when the website shows topics for the same search. Do not build a pipeline that requires them.

Can I get related queries for more than five keywords at once?

The unofficial explore endpoint still groups terms in small batches. A hosted client that paces sessions can walk a longer list and give you one row per keyword.
